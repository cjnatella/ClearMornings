            Day one, for the record

            On day one you gave up a night's fee you needed, because you could not face standing in a pub. Nobody asked you to.

            Keep that. It is the first piece of evidence in this attempt, and it cost you something, which is why it counts.

            For a Friday at five

            The week is done, and some part of you is already walking towards the first drink. It will call itself a reward, or a rest, or nothing much. It has said the same thing every time.

            You know how this film ends, because you have sat through it since the summer. The first drink switches off the person who makes your decisions. What follows is Sunday, and the inventory.

            So decide nothing tonight. The decision was made on a Sunday in November, by you, hungover and completely clear about it. Tonight you only have to carry it as far as bed. There is a plan on this page for exactly these hours. Do the next thing on it.

            Tomorrow you will wake early with a whole Saturday that belongs to you. Most of what you want gets built out of mornings like that.

            For the day you think you can manage it

            This thought arrives when things are going well, which is what makes it persuasive. You feel steady. The bad weekends seem a long way back. One drink looks like something a steady person could have.

            You have already run this experiment. A sober stretch of months ended on exactly this thought, and the result has come back the same every time since. You do not drink a little. Treat that as a finding and build on it, the way you would with any other reliable result.

            Feeling steady is what not drinking produces. It is the return on the decision, and it stops the moment the decision does.

            For an evening that feels empty

            Some evenings the flat is quiet and the quiet starts to sound like a verdict on you. It is only an evening.

            Drinking never fixed this. It pushed the feeling back a few hours and returned it on Sunday with interest. Sober, the feeling stays the size it actually is, and that is a size you can sit next to.

            Remember the spring. You forced none of it. You stopped drinking, capacity came back, and the gym, the money and the applications followed on their own. That is what you are like with a clear head.

            You are building something slowly and on purpose. A quiet evening is part of the building. Message one person, eat properly, and let it be an ordinary night.
